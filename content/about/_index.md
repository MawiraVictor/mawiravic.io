Mawira Victor

I am a Computer Networks and Security student with a focused interest in network architecture, cybersecurity, and system hardening. My goal is to design and implement secure, scalable, and efficient network solutions.

Through my degree program at Strathmore, I have developed a deep understanding of how data is transmitted and protected across networks. My interest in cybersecurity began with participation in CTF competitions, which evolved into a dedicated pursuit of penetration testing and network defense strategies.

I am a strong advocate of hands-on learning. I regularly build laboratory environments to simulate real-world scenarios, conduct controlled security assessments, and document my findings. Beyond technical exploration, I contribute to open-source security tools and mentor fellow students interested in the field.
